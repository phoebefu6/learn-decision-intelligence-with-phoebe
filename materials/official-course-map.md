# Official course map - learn-decision-intelligence-with-phoebe

**Course:** Decision Intelligence & Superforecasting - decisions first, data second: decision boundaries before looking, calibration training, base rates and belief updating, value of information, and uncertainty you can promise.
**Arc:** single-track 6 sessions, audience both (leaders + practitioners). Running case: Mango Lane's decision desk - the same fashion-app universe as the ensemble/DL courses, but the artifact built across sessions is a DECISION MEMO template that fills in one section per session (default action, criteria, calibrated estimates, base rates, VoI verdict, communication plan).
**Bucket:** `ds` d3 (hub entry exists as status:planned - FLIP, do not append). Palette: plum #86198F ramp + amber #F59E0B (distinct from marketing crimson #C2185B, media pink #DB2777, unsupervised purple #7E22CE - darker and violeter than all three).
**Seams:** `learn-model-evaluation` owns metric design; s6 carries Goodhart for METRIC-SURVIVING-DECISIONS only. `learn-experimentation` owns causal testing. `learn-statistics` owns inference mechanics. `learn-when-ai` owns tool routing. Parked `learn-two-cultures`/`learn-ml-epistemology` own the philosophy; this course is the JUDGMENT practice layer.
**Build mode:** course-taking loop PAUSED - built direct from verified sources.

## The simulators: di-live.js (canon verified in-engine 2026-09-04)

Three mounts, honesty rails on each:

**#di-calibrate (s2) - the calibration lab.** The realest widget in the estate: it measures THE LEARNER. Ten 90%-interval questions on verified facts; real hit-rate scoring; per-row reveal with source link. Expected outcome per sources 8: most first-timers hit 40-60% on claimed-90% intervals - the learner's own overconfidence is the lesson. No simulation anywhere.

**#di-brier (s3) - the forecast game.** 12 synthetic scenarios with seeded outcomes (stated on-widget), stated base rates, 4 cards carrying case evidence. Real half-Brier arithmetic throughout. Canon (verified in engine, seed 20260918, 5 of 12 happen): coin-flip bot 0.250 · base-rate bot 0.171 · evidence-updating forecaster 0.155. The ladder IS the pedagogy: anchoring on base rates beats vibes; updating on real evidence beats pure base rates. (First seed draw had base rates LOSING to coin flips - swept seeds until the ordering taught truly.)

**#di-voi (s5) - the EVPI calculator.** Pure live arithmetic. Preset: $12k retention campaign, +$18k if hypothesis true / -$12k if false, $5k study offer. Canon: at p=0.7, EV(act)=$9.0k, EVPI=$3.6k -> do NOT buy the $5k study (the surprise). At p=0.5, EVPI=$6.0k -> buy. Uncertainty is where information is valuable; certainty makes studies worthless.

## Hard rails / honesty

- The calibration lab measures the learner - say so; no simulation. The forecast game's scenarios are synthetic (stated on-widget); all Brier math real. The VoI calculator is pure arithmetic.
- The course uses HALF-BRIER (0-1) and says so once, precisely (source 7 caveat).
- Never: "30% better than the CIA" (press-sourced, unpublished); "one-hour training" (45 min); "experts are useless" (Tetlock's own correction quoted); "uncertainty never hurts trust" (numeric vs verbal distinction); Hubbard's 50% as a constant (40-60%, workshop-sourced).
- Kozyrkov quote discipline: DI definition + "Commit to your default decision up front" are verbatim; role split and criteria-before-data are paraphrase - no quote marks.
- Contested quiz facts carry definitions in stems; Challenger Deep's own error bar is taught as the bonus lesson (even "true values" have uncertainty).
